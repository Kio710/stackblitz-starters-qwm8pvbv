03PERSONAL PROJECT · POWER BI

Executive Financial Dashboard — PT Unilever Indonesia Tbk (IDX: UNVR)

A dummy executive dashboard tracking core performance metrics across FY2021–FY2025, built end-to-end from raw financial statements into an interactive report.

Power BI · Power Query · DAXFY2021 – FY2025Public financial statements
What I did
  • Extracted data from publicly available UNVR financial statements and modeled it specifically for this portfolio exercise.
  • Practiced the end-to-end Power BI workflow: cleaning raw financial data in Power Query, modeling financial ratios with DAX, and designing a clean, user-friendly report interface.
  • Designed the dashboard to highlight liquidity, profitability, and solvency at a glance for an executive audience.
Key takeaways from the data
  • Capital Structure: UNVR operates with notably high leverage — Debt to Equity peaked at 6.5 in 2024 before dropping to 3.5 in 2025.
  • Margin Stability: Gross Margins stayed resilient, consistently hovering between 46% and 49.7% despite the high leverage.
  • Liquidity: Current Ratio remained below 1.0 throughout the observation period, highlighting an operational strategy reliant on higher short-term liabilities.
